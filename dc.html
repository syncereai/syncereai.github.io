Data Collector

About the Role:

We are building intelligent home robots that can manipulate the real world with precision and reliability. High-quality data is essential to making this possible. As a Data Collector, you will use our provided hardware to record the motion and camera data that trains our robots, starting with laundry-folding tasks.

What You'll Do:

• Use the provided hardware to collect high-quality motion and camera data for household chores (starting with laundry folding).
• Attend a training session with our engineers to learn the exact data-collection process and standards.
• Follow task instructions clearly and precisely to ensure consistent, usable data.
• Meet your assigned quota of data-collection hours per week.
• Log and submit your data according to internal procedures.
• Maintain confidentiality and sign an NDA before beginning work.


What We're Looking For:

• Genuine excitement about robotics and its role in the home.
• Strong attention to detail and consistency when following procedures.
• Clear communication skills; fluent in English.
• Able to meet quotas and deadlines without reminders.


Nice to Have:

• Access to varied laundry (e.g., if you live with family) to provide diverse demonstrations.
• A home with multiple rooms or different environments, allowing varied data collection backgrounds.
• Experience with robotics, teleoperation, or knowledge of how robots learn from data.
• Physically able to perform household tasks (e.g., lifting up to 50 lbs).
• If you know other reliable people who could excel in this role, we will give your application priority (potential growth into management role).

Details:

• Full-time: 40–60 hours/week
• Part-time: Minimum 20 hours/week
• Fully remote
• Opportunities for advancement, including team lead/manager roles as we scale

How to Apply:

Send your resume and a cover letter with bullet points explaining why you’re the perfect candidate for this role (highlighting relevant experiences, projects, and skills). If someone referred you, please include their name in the application.

If there's something unique about you, please mention it. The clearer and more concise, the better 🙂. Make sure to mention your expected start date.

Send application to: hello@syncereai.com
Subject: Data Collector Application
